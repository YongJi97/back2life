About the studio

A fresh chapter for a neighborhood favorite.

Back 2 Life Studio just reopened under new management — same great chair, sharper than ever. Step in and you’ll see why people are talking: a hand-built honeycomb LED ceiling, ring-lit stations at every chair, and a team that takes pride in every tape up, fade, and design.

Whether you’re after a quick line-up before work, full braids, your kid’s first cut, or a hot towel reset on the weekend — we’ve got the chair, and we’ve got the time.

  • Walk-ins welcome — appointments recommended on weekends
  • Fades, tape ups, mohawks, flat tops, designs
  • Braids and hair extensions on-site
  • Kid-friendly — first cut, every cut
